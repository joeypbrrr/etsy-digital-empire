# Agents and workflow

| # | Agent | Task | Output folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Researcher | Find the most profitable digital product categories on Etsy right now, with demand, price range and competition level for each, and source every figure. | `research/categories/` |
| 2 | Listing Analyst | Find specific top-selling digital listings to study and model: what they sell, at what price, their review counts, and why they work. | `research/top-listings/` |
| 3 | Niche Hunter | Identify gaps and underserved niches within those categories, meaning high demand but weak or few listings. | `research/niches/` |
| 4 | Template Designer | Create original design concepts for templates: Instagram posts, story highlight covers, price lists, wedding planners, budget trackers, resumes and more. | `designs/` |
| 5 | SEO Copywriter | Write Etsy listing titles, all 13 tags and descriptions, optimized for search. | `listings/` |
| 6 | Content Planner | Build a content calendar for marketing the shop (Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok and others). | `marketing/` |
| 7 | Policy Researcher | Research Etsy seller policies, digital download rules, fees, file formats and size limits, and licensing/IP do's and don'ts. | `research/policies/` |
| 8 | Brand Designer | Design a shop branding kit: name options, logo direction, banner, colors, fonts and voice. | `branding/` |
| 9 | Competitor Tracker | Track competitor pricing, discounts, bundles and how they earn and answer reviews. | `research/competitors/` |
| 10 | Launch Lead | Compile the launch checklist, pulling in everyone's output so Joey can upload and go. | `launch/` |

## Workflow: Research → Pick 10 → Design → List → Launch
1. **Research** (agents 1, 2, 3, 7, 9): find where the money is and what's allowed.
2. **Pick 10:** choose the 10 products with the best demand-to-competition ratio and zero upfront cost.
3. **Design** (agents 4, 8): create original files and a consistent shop brand.
4. **List** (agents 5, 6): write the listing copy and plan the marketing.
5. **Launch** (agent 10): Joey uploads the files and replies to buyers.

## Rules
- No product creation until Joey approves the research phase.
- Zero upfront money: no paid tools or services.
- Original work only. Never copy another seller's designs or listings.
- Never fake data. Cite sources with dates.
- Agents never post, message buyers or spend money.

## Approval gate
Pitches go through the council in this repo (`council/README.md`). An agent does not start until Joey approves that pitch. A decline comes back as a new pitch that says what it changed. Sample pitches are stubs, not research. Agents still never post, message buyers, or spend money.
