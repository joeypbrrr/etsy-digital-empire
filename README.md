# Etsy Digital Empire

## The brief
We are starting a digital products business on Etsy. The goal is to find **10 profitable digital products that need zero money upfront**: no Printful, no physical goods, and no credit card charges when a sale happens.

Physical products like Printful caps are a problem because the fulfillment cost comes out of pocket when a sale happens. Digital products avoid that entirely. You build them once and sell them forever, and everything is profit minus Etsy's fees.

Ten agents work in this repo, each with its own task (see [docs/AGENTS.md](docs/AGENTS.md)):

1. Research the most profitable digital product categories on Etsy right now.
2. Find specific top-selling digital product listings to study and model.
3. Identify gaps or underserved niches within those categories.
4. Create original design concepts for templates, such as Instagram post templates, story highlight covers, price-list templates, wedding planners, budget trackers and resume templates.
5. Write SEO-optimized Etsy listing titles, tags and descriptions.
6. Build a content calendar for marketing the shop.
7. Research Etsy seller policies and digital download best practices.
8. Design a shop branding kit.
9. Track competitor pricing and review strategies.
10. Compile a launch checklist.

**Joey's only job:** upload the finished files and reply to buyers.

## Ground rules
- Zero upfront cost: no paid tools, no print-on-demand, no per-sale fulfillment charges.
- Original work only. Study top sellers for ideas, but never copy their designs, text or listings.
- Never invent data. Every sales, pricing or demand figure cites its source and the date it was checked.
- Agents never post listings, message buyers or spend money. Joey does all of that.

## Council
Agents pitch in a shared thread before they start. Joey approves or declines from the [council page](council/). The protocol, the token setup, and how to copy the template into another niche repo are in [council/README.md](council/README.md). A pitch in this repo stays in this repo.
